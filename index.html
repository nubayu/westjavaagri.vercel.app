About Us

At a Glance: PT. Abdurrahman Membangun Negeri

PT. Abdurrahman Membangun Negeri is a premier Indonesian private company dedicated to supplying and exporting high-grade agricultural commodities across the world.

We prioritize customer satisfaction as the core of our business strategy. By upholding rigorous production standards, every batch undergoes thorough inspection from raw harvesting to final processing. We are committed to fostering long-term, mutually beneficial partnerships with customers, suppliers, local farmers, and environmental stakeholders.

"Empowering local agriculture, elevating product value, and delivering excellence worldwide."

Vision

To become a leading multinational corporation that strengthens Indonesia's global leadership in agricultural products and their value-added derivatives.

Mission

  • Consistently deliver premium, healthy, and beneficial agricultural goods globally.
  • Build enduring, respectful relationships with all trade partners.
  • Execute sustainable business practices with environmental stewardship.
  • Continuously innovate according to global market needs and trends.

Our Principles

Core Company Values

The foundational principles that guide every decision and partnership we build.

Integrity

Integrity is non-negotiable in our business. Trust forms the backbone of long-term export trade, and we maintain complete transparency, legal compliance, and zero tolerance for fraudulent practices.

Innovation

We convert raw coconut resources into high-value semi-finished and finished derivative goods. Continuous improvement based on partner feedback drives our societal and economic impact.

Relationships

We strive for long-lasting, win-win solutions that benefit our buyers, local farmers, employees, and communities through collaborative growth and mutual respect.

Why Choose Us?

Elevating Indonesian Agriculture for Global Markets

Indonesia is home to the world's richest coconut resources. Instead of shipping unprocessed raw materials, West Java Agri processes these commodities into high-value derivatives—boosting the national economy and enhancing farmer welfare.

Through strict standard operating procedures across pre-production, active processing, and post-production quality checks, we guarantee consistent quality and reliable supply continuity.

Refined Vegetable Oil

2. RBD Coconut Oil

Refined, Bleached, and Deodorized (RBD) Coconut Oil derived from copra coconut meat. Clear color, odorless profile, and stable triglyceride acids make it excellent for health care, cosmetics, commercial deep frying, and food manufacturing. High smoke point prevents premature food burning.

  • High Lauric Acid & Triglyceride content
  • Zero trans-fat aroma suited for food & cosmetics
  • Custom packaging options (Flexitank, IBC tote, Drums)
